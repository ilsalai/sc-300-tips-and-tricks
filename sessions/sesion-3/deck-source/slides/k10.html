La session policy no hace nada

A Salesforce session policy blocks downloads on unmanaged devices. Users still download. No CA policy targets it. Fix?

A

Connect Salesforce with an app connector

CA policy for Salesforce with Use Conditional Access App Control

C

CA policy for Salesforce with Use app enforced restrictions

D

Tag Salesforce as Sanctioned in the catalog

Por qué

Access y session policies necesitan que una política de CA mande la sesión a Defender for Cloud Apps: Use Conditional Access App Control, con Use custom policy.

A. El connector trabaja por API. No controla la sesión del browser.

C. Solo funciona con Exchange Online y SharePoint Online.

D. Sanctioned es una etiqueta de discovery. No enruta sesiones.
