Todos los Windows hybrid joined

You need a dynamic device group with every Windows device that is Microsoft Entra hybrid joined. Which deviceTrustType value do you use?

A

AzureAD

ServerAD

C

Workplace

D

HybridJoined

Por qué

ServerAD = hybrid joined. Va en un security group con membership Dynamic Device y la regla se escribe en el text box.

A. AzureAD son los Microsoft Entra joined (solo nube).

C. Workplace son los registered (BYOD).

D. No existe ese valor. Suena lógico y por eso es trampa.
